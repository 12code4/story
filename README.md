# NIGHTWARD

**Book One: The Walked** — a long-form sci-fi/horror/adventure story, written to be adapted into a video game.

This repository is the working home for the story: the world bible, the character dossiers, the full
chapter-by-chapter outline, and one treatment file per chapter that we refine over time.

## The pitch in five lines

A colony ship crash-landed 181 years ago on the night side of a tidally locked world. The survivors
walked out of the dark and built cities around geothermal vents in the twilight band. Now their
reactors are failing, and the only spare cores are still aboard the wreck — three thousand
kilometers into permanent night. For years, people have been sleepwalking toward the dark. Lately,
some of them have started coming back — and asking their loved ones to come with them.

An expedition sets out to salvage the cores. The story is the crossing: what it costs, who it
changes, and what is waiting at the ship.

## Design pillars

- **Adventure-shaped, not clue-shaped.** The plot engine is *go get the thing before home freezes*.
  The strange and the terrible are weather the expedition moves through, not a puzzle the reader is
  being dealt cards for. See `book1/06-deferred.md` for the mystery policy.
- **Dread over gore.** The dark never forces anyone. It invites. The scariest thing in this world is
  being asked nicely, in a voice you miss.
- **Heat is love.** Warmth is currency, health, community, and attention. Everything alive is kept
  alive by hands. This is the thematic spine and (later) the core game system.
- **Slow burn, earned people.** Six chapters pass before the expedition leaves the walls. Side-quest
  chapters are load-bearing: they are where the characters get built.
- **The weird has rules.** We (the authors) keep a private rule sheet for the phenomenon so the
  mindfuck stays consistent instead of arbitrary. Characters never learn the rules in Book One.

## How we work (chapter-to-chapter refinement)

Every chapter file has a **status**: `outline → treatment → draft → locked`. Everything currently
sits at **treatment** — enough beats to see the chapter, loose enough to change cheaply.

To steer the next session, say things like:

- "Refine chapter 9" → the treatment becomes scene-by-scene, with sample prose and dialogue.
- "Punch up Brakes" / "I don't buy Cistern's arc" → a character pass across all files.
- "Tone check Part III" → re-read and tighten a whole part.
- "Now let's do the mystery pass" → we open `06-deferred.md` and design the truth layer together.

Big structural changes (premise, protagonist, ending) are still cheap at this stage — nothing is
drafted as prose yet. Say the word and we pivot.
