# Book One: The Walked — Chapter Map

26 chapters, four parts. Each chapter has a full treatment file in `chapters/`. Status of all
chapters: **treatment** (beats fixed enough to see, loose enough to change cheaply).

Between every pair of chapters sits one **paper-trail document** (log, letter, writ, manifest) —
named in each chapter file. They carry the off-camera voices (home politics, Frame's pursuit,
Cistern's inner life) without breaking the single-POV camera.

---

## Part I — The Verge (chs. 1–6)
*Home, cast, stakes. Slow by design: the reader must love what the dark is near.*

1. **The Cold-Line** — Iyo's night shift on the line: the rote of recovery, the culture of lamps
   and counting; a routine save; the light thinner than the instruments admit. At shift's end,
   something on the line walking the *wrong way* — out of the dark. Elo Weir, six years lost,
   asking for Ude Kiln by rank.
2. **Hearthfall** — the city, daily life, Odile, Maren's chair; the returner processed; Order and
   Compact react; the Manifest of the Missing introduced. Texture chapter, deliberately.
3. **The Vault** — Ude Kiln: 260 years, the ship's history as he alone carries it; Heart Two's
   sour Hum; the reserve cores; the salvage case argued before the Compact. Iyo refuses to go —
   until Elo says, in Maren's exact voice, *"you counted wrong."* The vote passes by one.
4. **Mustering** — the Column assembled member by member, each met in their element (Brakes in
   the debt-pen shop, Adder at drill, Cistern's infirmary-chapel, Solene's mast, Tapper in the
   Vault cellars). Families bring names for the Manifest of the Missing. What you pack to leave
   the light.
5. **The Last Vent** — departure rites; past the final farm terrace; first camp beyond warmth;
   watch rotations, Kiln's command style tested by a teamster's cold-injury (turn back or go on).
   Elo sits the night watch "so the others don't have to."
6. **Gloaming** — walking out of daytime forever; the first Station of the Walk; Cistern's
   liturgy at the Founders' graves; the psychological threshold of lamp-life. First deniable
   wrongness: cindermoths won't come to their lamps. End of Part I.

## Part II — The Grey Reach (chs. 7–12)
*Archaeology of their own trauma; the human dangers of the Reach; the party becomes a unit.*

7. **The Reach** — the Founders' retreat path read in reverse: camps, caches, graves, panic
   fossilized as artifact. Cache ethics. Provender signs. Kiln's first slip (calls Solene by a
   dead crewmate's name).
8. **Saltcamp** — Mother Quill's market: heat by the hour, courtesy absolute; the caged Walked
   ("stock"); the Column's first ethics fracture (buy one free? intervene? mission-first?).
   Learning the Reach's real economy.
9. **Lighthouse 9** *(side quest)* — the last crewed relay; keeper Anser, eleven years alone,
   logs that record the dark "practicing." Brakes fixes the great lens; Solene relieves the
   watch for one night. His requested payment: carry his relief application home. Restraint horror.
10. **Curfew** *(side quest — Adder's chapter)* — Cinder Row, the town Adder helped freeze. Its
    doors still keep the bell schedule; its dead walk their rounds, rehearsing ordinary errands.
    Adder's confession, his attempted rite of amends; one of the walkers pauses — and nods. No
    combat. The party leaves changed; Adder leaves lighter and worse.
11. **The Glass Fields** — the Errand's debris corridor; hull-shard labyrinth; scav gangs; the
    Provender toll-bridge over the first rift. The haggling goes wrong: **Pell is killed** —
    human, close, stupid. Field funeral. Solene intercepts the Compact's recall writ on the
    wind: home has turned; a Warden is coming.
12. **The Reachgate** — the Founders' arch at the cliff edge; the planned rotation turns four
    teamsters home with honor; the writ debated and burned by Kiln. In the night, **Corun
    walks** — and Iyo's recovery *fails on the page*: he negotiates in Pell's voice; she loses
    the count. Gone. The Column passes under the arch. End of Part II.

## Part III — The Sea of Static (chs. 13–18)
*Isolation, storms, pursuit; the midpoint disaster; the stranger; lights where none can be.*

13. **Landfall in Reverse** — down the cliffs to the frozen sea ("the Founders came up these
    stairs"); black ice, pressure ridges, the first under-ice knocks; radio dies. Lighthouse 9
    has gone silent behind them. Solene hails home and *her own voice* answers.
14. **The Anchorage** — the fallen cargo tether: a 40 km steel road strung with intact pods —
    manifests of the life never lived (schoolhouse kits, orchard stock). **Frame catches them**:
    the standoff on the cable; Vell defects to the law; a sky-tear forces both parties into one
    pod. Enemies under one roof.
15. **Sky-Tear** — the storm chapter, interior and hallucinatory: each character's private
    wrongness on the page (the counting; the work-song; the bell; broadcasts from last year;
    Kiln remembering the descent from *outside* the ship). Storm passes: one of Frame's troopers
    has walked. Her certainty cracks; the truce is struck — both columns nightward now.
16. **The Lead** — the great open-water crack; Brakes' bridge; the Benthos surfaces once —
    vast, indifferent, drinking their lamplight. The crossing costs **Kettle** (the crawler,
    their rolling warmth) and **Old Say** — the water, then the warm tent, then mid-sentence
    gone. Midpoint night: the Column at its coldest, in every sense.
17. **The Long Cold** — attrition march; hunger math; the discontinuities multiply (their own
    footprints joining the trail, Pell's knife back in its sheath). Out of the dark walks
    **Ghost**: accurate maps, wrong joints, seen differently by every eye; he trades maps for
    company. Frame wants him arrested; he finds this delightful.
18. **Farside Light** — the Vein: a warm seam under the ice, lantern reefs rising, the sound of
    — impossibly — children, carried over the ice. Across the dark: a lit harbor. Elo, for the
    first time, walks in front. End of Part III.

## Part IV — Night Country (chs. 19–26)
*The settlement that shouldn't exist; the ship; the racks; the gift, the question, the return.*

19. **Farside Landing** — arrival and hospitality; the Stayers' culture: returners at dinner,
    walking as ordination, "we know where everyone is." The Column's categories break politely.
    Farside's one rule: *take nothing from the ship she doesn't hand you.* Iyo scans every face
    for Maren; she is not here.
20. **The Warming** *(side quest-ish)* — Farside's festival; the Column embedded in ordinary
    Stayer life for one breath (Brakes and the machine-songs; Adder and Reeve Stay's untold war;
    Solene in Mirin's radio room, where home arrives with no static at all). **Cistern walks** —
    with a letter, as ministry. The Column grieves; Farside congratulates. The book's worst
    dinner party.
21. **The Approach** — the last leg, Farside guides, the wreck seen at last: the Errand across
    the Keelbreak, city-sized, lit faintly from within. The paths carry *traffic* — the Walked,
    generations of them, busy and courteous. Kiln failing, naming her parts like a lover as they
    come up the road. Frame's remaining troopers make camp outside; she goes on.
22. **The Errand** — aboard: the hold; **the racks** — 20,000 sleepers, alive, tended. **Maren**,
    mid-task, kind, *busy*: "I can't now — we're almost done counting." The ship's gift: two
    Hearts, freely handed over. **Kiln dies on the command deck**, logged home by his ship, the
    Walked standing watch — honor or acquisition, no answer. They take the cargo and no answers.
23. **What Was Asked** — the counterweight: Elo puts the formal question to the whole Column —
    *anyone may stay.* Who hesitates (Solene; Brakes' hands say yes while her mouth says no);
    Iyo asks for Maren instead and is gently refused ("she's needed — so are you, somewhere
    else"). Frame reads the roster *out* — bureaucracy as exorcism. The ship recalls Tapper;
    Tapper, permitted for once to weigh an order, taps NO and walks out. Elo stays: delivered.
24. **The Return** — compressed: Farside's escort to the Anchorage; the dark grants safe passage
    — which lands as worse than any pursuit; Quill waives the toll ("first refusal on whatever
    you didn't bring back"); home politics somersault; **Lantern relit on the page** — bells,
    weeping, the full adventure payoff, earned and undercut in the same hour.
25. **The Manifest of the Missing** — the accounting: door to door with the book of names,
    telling families what their lost now are ("alive; not coming back; ... employed"). The Order
    splits; the Compact debates Farside; Frame resigns to found the Line Watch with Iyo; Brakes
    pardoned, keeps Tapper; Odile's question: *"Is she happy?"* — "I don't know. She's busy."
26. **Nightward** *(coda)* — months later. The light is still thinning; nothing is fixed; the
    relationship is different: linewalkers carry Farside protocols, some walkers come back on
    their own, everyone keeps a lamp in the window facing the dark. Elo seen once at the
    cold-line — facing *homeward*, waving. Iyo walks her line and her step-count comes home one
    high; no one behind her. She latches the door, leaves the lamp lit. End of Book One.
