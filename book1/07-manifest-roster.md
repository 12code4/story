# The Manifest of the Missing — Canonical Roster

The carried book is the story's holiest object and the game's quest log. This file is the
single source of truth for its individualized entries: who they are, what token they carry,
where they resolve, and which door answers for them in ch. 25. The case holds **214 names**
(families chose to enter them at the ch. 4 muster); the book individualizes the entries below;
the rest are "the long tail" — read at ch. 23, answered, unnamed on the page.

Rule (from ch. 23, locked): at the roster-reading, **not one name goes unanswered.** Statuses
are terse — *present; working; well* — with exactly one strange answer (Corm Wick's). The game
may leave names unresolved in the player's log; the story's answer exists regardless — "we
don't know" is a player deliverable, not a world state.

## Game notes

- The roster = the quest database. Each individualized entry above is a quest chain with a
  muster scene (ch. 4), zero or more road beats, a census state (ch. 22), a roster answer
  (ch. 23), and a door (ch. 25). The long tail generates census/roster/door content
  procedurally from a name+year+token table (write ~40 filler entries at production).
- Tokens are physical props on Kettle's case, then on the hand-sledges after ch. 16. The
  case's wax seal is broken on-page exactly twice: ch. 12 (Corun's amendment) and ch. 23
  (the reading).
- Door-generation rule (ch. 25): resolved → found-voice answer plays; unresolved → the
  "we don't know" wheel with one spoke. The seven authored doors are mandatory bosses.
