# Chapter 17 — The Long Cold

**Part III: The Sea of Static** · **Status: treatment** · Weeks nineteen–twenty-one. The deep
Sea, on foot, without Kettle. Ghost joins.

**Function**: The attrition chapter — hunger math, carried heat, the Column at its physical
floor — with the discontinuities quietly multiplying, and then the book's strangest character
walking out of the dark to trade maps for company.

## Beats

1. **The arithmetic of carried warmth.** Life after Kettle, rendered procedurally: stove
   rotation by pairs; wet-boot triage; the allowance recomputed by Vell across the truce
   line (his tables now serve both columns — the yoked body has one stomach). The march
   is the slowest sustained suffering in the book and the prose should go spare with it:
   short chapters-within-the-chapter, distances, temperatures, who carried what. The
   wardens' second machine dies (bearings; cold past its rating) and is cannibalized
   without ceremony — and with it goes Frame's reach: she details five of her troopers
   home under a corporal, carrying duplicate logs and the last machine-fuel margin, and
   keeps three. Nobody calls it retreat; it is load-shedding, and by now everyone on the
   Sea can read the strata. Everyone's world contracts to load, lamp, next camp. This is
   where the book earns the right to its ending: the reader must do the crossing on foot.
2. **The discontinuities.** Against that spare register, the Sea's small bookkeeping —
   three instances, spaced, each once (rule sheet), each landing on a different witness:
   - Camp is struck one morning and the outbound line of march crosses **a set of prints
     joining their own from the right** — matching the Column's boot-issue, correct
     stride, falling into file two positions behind Iyo — that simply *begin*, mid-Sea,
     and continue with them until the noon halt, after which no one can find where they
     stopped. Adder logs boot-issue and stride. No count of the walking column, taken
     while walking, misses or gains anyone. (Iyo does not take one. Noted.)
   - **Pell's knife** — buried in his hands at the Glass Fields, moved to Corun's
     Manifest line at the Reachgate — is found by Dovie in the twins' tool-roll on the
     sledge, oiled, wrapped, where it always lived. Dovie brings it to Iyo, wordless.
     They put it back in the roll. (What else is there to do with it? The book asks the
     question by not asking it.)
   - Solene's board, mid-band, dead-of-watch: **four seconds of her own hail from
     ch. 13** — her voice, her call-sign, her exact weariness — played back at her from
     nightward. She logs it this time. The entry (paper trail) is one line and it is the
     bravest sentence she has written.
3. **Ghost.** Day fifty-something on the Sea, visibility a lamp's throw: the forward
   watch calls a light — steady, low, *approaching* — and out of the dark walks a man.
   Alone, unhurried, hauling a small neat sledge, dressed in gear none of them can place
   (good gear; wrong lineage — seams in the wrong places, as if convergently evolved).
   He hails them by — this lands on everyone at once — *lamp etiquette*: stops at the
   correct distance, uncovers his light twice. Verge line-courtesy, six hundred
   kilometers from the Verge. He gives no name ("names are for rosters; I'm not
   joining"), answers to Cartographer, then — Brakes, day two, inevitably — to Ghost.
   He is heading the way they came ("someone should update the far shore's charts; the
   storm moved your coast a little"), he has maps — hand-drawn, annotated, and (the
   scouts confirm against ranged terrain) *accurate*, including the Lead's Narrows,
   including a warm-seam route to the sea's far shore labeled in a tidy hand THE VEIN —
   and he will trade copies for exactly one price: **company**. "Walk me in your line a
   while. The Sea's poor conversation."
4. **The problem of his face.** He is entirely pleasant and the Column cannot agree what
   he looks like. The book does this in dialogue only, once (tone rule 2), in camp, out
   of his hearing: Adder, filing his security note, gives height/build for the record
   and Solene corrects him — laughing, sure he's joking — and Cistern's third
   description agrees with neither, and Iyo, who has been looking at Ghost across the
   fire the whole exchange, declines to give one. The moment passes as tired people
   being tired. It is never raised again. (Guardrail check: no one compiles the
   anomaly; the reader does. Frame — beat, not scene — solves it as a warden would:
   her file note reads *"description: declined."*)
5. **What he's for.** Days of him in the line, and the chapter banks his function:
   he is *good company* — funny in an unplaceable register, full of the Sea's practical
   lore (which camp-ice sings false; how to bank a stove against a draw-wind "so you
   spend less light"), respectful of the dead when they pass the wardens' dead machine
   ("everything that worked hard deserves a marker"), and utterly, restfully
   incurious about the Column's errand. The one exchange the chapter must land, Iyo
   and Ghost, rear of the line, lamps bobbing ahead: she asks where Farside Landing is
   (the name from his map's margin). He tells her the bearing. She asks what it's like.
   He considers the question with real care and says: "Kept." Then, kindly, nodding
   up the line at the Column's lamps: "Like that. You keep it as you go, or you don't
   arrive." End chapter on the yoked column plus one, walking; and the last line given
   to Frame's field log, deadpan warden prose doing the book's work: *"Day 54. Guide
   attached. Provenance: none. Charts: correct. This command notes that correct is
   not the same as good."*

## Character tracking

- **Ghost**: on stage. Speech register locked (courteous, concrete, no questions about
  the mission, no answers about himself, gnomic only when asked gnomic questions).
  He stays through ch. 19, remains at Farside, gifts the true maps ch. 24. Parked file
  owns everything else about him.
- **Iyo**: declining to describe him — her linewalker's instinct now operating past the
  rote's edge; she has begun *extending professional courtesy to the unexplained*, and
  she knows it, and the book lets her be disquieted by her own adaptation.
- **Solene**: logging the playback = her integrity reasserted, at cost. The unlogged
  ch. 15 entry still stands between her and her book; ch. 20 will close that loop.
- **Brakes**: naming him Ghost; teaching him the stove-bank trick back after he
  improves hers — her first new-made joke since the Lead (recovery's first green
  shoot, timed deliberately small).
- **Vell**: the one-stomach tables — the defector now literally feeding the body he
  informed on. Nobody thanks him. He'd refuse it.

## The dread beat

The prints that join and keep file. (The knife and the playback are close seconds, but
the prints walking *in formation* — the dark observing march discipline, or the march
absorbing the dark's — is Part III's thesis in one image.)

## Paper trail (interstitial after this chapter)

**Solene's radio log, one line**: *"0311: own hail (day 6 arch.) returned, bearing
nightward, 4s. Logged in full. — S.A."* — followed, facing page, by Ghost's map of the
Vein route, redrawn in her hand for the navigation folder, the tidy original label THE
VEIN copied exactly, and beneath it her one annotation, the chapter's held breath:
*"his ink was fresher than his paper."*

## Game notes

The survival stretch: systems at full weight (carried heat, load management, stove
rotation as party scheduling). The three discontinuities are hand-authored events per
the sanctioned system: prints (world-geometry event the player can walk beside and
measure), knife (inventory event — it reappears in the stack; no notification), playback
(radio event at the nightly ritual). Ghost joins as an escort-guest NPC whose barks are
the reward for bringing him (party members describe him inconsistently in ambient
dialogue only — never in menus; his codex portrait slot is the one silhouette in the
game). His maps update the player's chart UI with hand-drawn overlays — diegetic
fast-knowledge, not fast-travel.

## Open questions

  craft, in her register; guardrail-test it with cold readers. Candidate trim: cut the
  annotation, keep the redrawn label. The map itself is already the wrongness.)
- Does Ghost interact with Elo? (Current: one beat, no dialogue — they pass on the
  line, and Ghost gives him the two-uncover lamp courtesy, and Elo returns it. Two
  professionals. Nothing else, ever. Anything more collapses both.)
- The prints' file-position (two behind Iyo): keep the specificity? (Yes. Specific and
  unexplained beats vague and atmospheric — house style.)
