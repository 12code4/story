# Chapter 12 — The Reachgate

**Part II: The Grey Reach** · **Status: expanded treatment** · Week twelve. The Founders' arch at the
cliff edge above the frozen sea. Part II finale.

**Function**: Threshold, rotation, and the book's first on-page failed recovery. The rote breaks;
the count breaks; Iyo's competence — the reader's one certainty — takes structural damage. The
Column passes the point where the Founders' story and theirs stop rhyming and start reversing.

## Beats

1. **The arch.** The Reachgate: the Founders' monument at the head of the great stair they cut
   in the cliffs, 181 years ago, climbing *out*. Ship-plate and dressed stone; the inscription
   in crash-era lettering: **WE CARRIED FIRE OUT OF THE NIGHT.** Below it, wind-worn, the
   tally of the Walk's survivors as first carved (1,407) and — Kiln shows them, low on the
   left pillar, uncommented in any record — a second, smaller line in a different hand:
   *"and left it burning behind us."* The Column stands with the sea wind coming up the
   cliff, the Sea of Static running out beyond the world's edge, and reads their own errand
   in reverse on their ancestors' monument.
2. **The rotation.** The planned turn-back, honored to the letter because after chs. 8–11
   everyone understands what letters are worth: four go home — Marta (healed enough to walk,
   not to cross a sea), Harrow, Sung, Ferro — escorting Tam, carrying the mail (families'
   letters, Anser's application, Vell's sealed pouch — *the reader clocks the pouch*), and
   duplicate copies of every log. The goodbyes are done in the Column's grammar: tools
   exchanged, loads re-rigged, no speeches. Marta takes Pell's spare gloves to his and
   Corun's mother. The four-and-Tam diminish up-trail into the grey, and the Column watches
   until they're gone, and the book lets that be as bad as it is: the last people who can
   still turn around, turning around.
3. **The writ, answered.** At the arch, in sight of the sea, Kiln formally acknowledges the
   Compact's broadcast — Solene keys the mast for the last high-power window this side of the
   crossing — and his reply is entered in the log verbatim (the paper trail after this
   chapter): receipt confirmed; recall *declined*, on the authority of the freight ledger
   (the cores are ship's cargo; he is ship's officer; the Compact's writ, he notes with
   perfect courtesy, does not extend to another vessel's manifest); position appended;
   *"Column proceeds. Kiln, W.O., CV Errand."* Then he burns the writ's transcribed copy at
   the arch — not defiance-theater for the Column (though it is that) but something older:
   an officer clearing his desk. Vell watches the paper burn with a face like a man doing
   long division.
4. **Corun walks.** That night, the last camp above the stair. Iyo, on watch, catches the
   motion she has caught forty-one times: Corun, bootless, past the lamp-ring, walking with
   the sea-wind full in his face. The rote deploys — lit side, name, left hand — and the
   book's engine seizes: he *negotiates*. In Pell's voice — Pell's exact cadence, the twins'
   patter running one-sided — cheerful, reasonable, walking: *"we're only going down to look,
   Iyo; you can count us both back after."* She takes the hand; she counts; and somewhere in
   the sixties the count **comes apart** — she loses the number *while saying it*, the way a
   word dissolves when repeated, and knows with total bodily certainty she has never once
   known what the numbers were for. The hand in hers is patient. Then it isn't there, and
   the lamp-ring is forty paces behind her, not the four she'd counted, and Corun Dray is
   a diminishing figure on the great stair, going down into the dark of the sea with his
   brother's voice, and she is standing on the top step with the next number in her mouth
   and no way to say it. Adder finds her there. She has not moved. She is holding, very
   hard, the number she stopped on. (We never print the number.)
5. **Under the arch.** Dawn-bell. No pursuit — the stair in the dark is death, and everyone
   including Iyo knows Corun is not on the stair anymore. The Column re-rigs for descent in
   silence. Cistern amends the Manifest of the Missing in his best hand: *Corun Dray. Y.181.
   Brother (deceased). Token: —* and then, because the twins shared everything, moves Pell's
   knife-entry to Corun's line. The Column passes under the Reachgate — the reversed
   benediction Elo murmured at the first Station (ch. 6, if kept) now simply true: they are
   carrying fire *into* the night. Last image: from the first switchback, the arch above
   them against the last of the Reach's grey light, and Elo pausing under it a half-second
   longer than anyone, like a man touching a mezuzah. End Part II.

## Character tracking

- **Iyo**: the load-bearing break. Her rote — the book's one reliable magic — has failed on
  the page, in her hands, on a friend. Track the aftermath small and long: she stops
  counting aloud on watch (the Column notices the silence); she recounts her 41 recoveries
  in her head at night and can no longer remember if it was 41. The number-doubt is hers
  now. (Rule sheet: the dark is exact about consent — Corun *answered*. No one, including
  the reader, gets told this.)
- **Kiln**: the declination — his finest formal hour; also the moment he stops being the
  Compact's and becomes only the ship's. Watch his pronouns after this: "we" means the
  Errand.
- **Vell**: the burned writ + the sealed pouch gone home = his position now impossible; ch. 14
  resolves it.
- **Corun** out. The twins' arc complete: the road took one with violence and one with a
  voice.

## The dread beat

The negotiation, and specifically its *reasonableness* — and the count dissolving from the
inside. This is the book's first full demonstration that the dark's courtesy has been, all
along, the courtesy of something that was never being kept out — only *declining to come in*.

## Paper trail (interstitial after this chapter)

**Column log, Reachgate entries**: Kiln's declination verbatim (officer's prose, immaculate);
beneath it the watch log for the night in Adder's hand — *"0300: C. Dray lost to the night.
Recovery attempted by I. Ballast. No fault found."* — and the last line, added by a different
pen, unattributed, the book's quietest gut-punch: *"No fault found"* underlined. Twice.

## Game notes

Act finale. The rotation is a real farewell scene with inventory consequence (loads
redistribute; the player physically hands over letters). The declination can be a dialogue
where the player keys the mast. Corun's walk is the mechanic-betrayal beat designed in
`05-game-notes.md`: the recovery minigame runs — and the count UI itself corrupts (digits
wrong, rhythm window drifting) while Pell's voice plays warm and close; the failure is
authored, unavoidable, and the game must never acknowledge the UI corruption afterward
(count-lie #1 of 2). Descend the great stair as the act-break traversal: one continuous
descent, the Reach's grey light going out above, the Sea's black opening below.

## Expanded treatment — content pass

**Decisions taken this pass**: Kiln's declination printed in full (locked — it is forty words
and it is his character in amber). The watch-log underline stays forever unattributed (locked).

### Scene list

- **S1 — The arch** (Reachgate, cliff head · all). WE CARRIED FIRE OUT OF THE NIGHT; the
  survivor tally (1,407); the smaller second line ("and left it burning behind us"). The sea
  wind coming up the cliff. Turn: reading their own errand in reverse on their ancestors'
  monument.
- **S2 — The rotation** (the arch camp · all). Four home with Tam: Marta, Harrow, Sung, Ferro.
  Tools exchanged, loads re-rigged, no speeches; Marta takes Pell's spare gloves for the Dray
  mother; the mail and Vell's sealed pouch (the reader clocks the pouch). Turn: watching the
  last people who can still turn around, turning around.
- **S3 — The answer** (the mast, last high-power window · Kiln, Solene, all). Receipt
  confirmed; recall declined on the freight ledger's authority; position appended; "Column
  proceeds. Kiln, W.O., CV Errand." He burns the transcribed writ at the arch — an officer
  clearing his desk. Turn: Vell watching the paper burn, doing long division.
- **S4 — Corun** (past the lamp-ring, night · Iyo, Corun; Adder arriving late). The rote
  deploys; the negotiation in Pell's voice ("we're only going down to look; you can count us
  both back after"); the count coming apart from the inside; forty paces that were four; the
  stair; gone. Turn: the number held in her mouth with no way to say it. (We never print it.)
- **S5 — Under the arch** (dawn-bell · all). No pursuit — everyone including Iyo knows he is
  not on the stair. Cistern amends the Manifest; Pell's knife-entry moved to Corun's line.
  The Column passes under; Elo pauses beneath it a half-second, like a man touching a
  mezuzah. End of Part II.

### Dialogue keys

- **Kiln** (the declination, in full, logged): "Receipt of Writ 4471-C acknowledged. Recall
  declined. The reserve cores are ship's cargo; I am ship's officer; the Compact's writ does
  not extend to another vessel's manifest. Position appended. Column proceeds. Kiln, W.O.,
  CV Errand."
- **Marta** (taking the gloves): "I'll tell her he wore through the first pair working." —
  the rotation's whole dignity in one planned kindness.
- **Corun** (in Pell's cadence, warm): "We're only going down to look, Iyo. You can count us
  both back after." — the book's engine seizing; reasonableness as horror.
- **Adder** (finding her on the top step, quiet): "Ballast. Inside the lamps." — not comfort,
  procedure; procedure *as* comfort, the only kind she could survive right then.
- **Elo** (under the arch, to no one, reversed benediction cadence): —no line printed;
  described only. (Locked: his mouth moves; the book does not transcribe it.)

### Game content pack

**Locations**: the Reachgate (arch, tally pillar, cliff head), the arch camp, the mast point,
the great stair head (the recovery-failure stage), the first switchbacks (Part III transition
descent).

**NPCs**: the rotation four + Tam (departure staging), otherwise the Column only. (The
chapter is deliberately unpeopled — the Reach is done giving them strangers.)

**Side quests / optional content**:
- *The Mail* — assemble the homebound pouch before the rotation leaves: collect letters from
  each Column member (each an optional one-scene conversation — what people say when they
  might be writing their last page home; write all eleven). Player carries them to Marta;
  the game confirms delivery in ch. 24's radio traffic.
- *The Tally Pillar* — optional close-read of the arch: the 1,407, the second line, and
  (interactable) the wind-worn edge where later hands added small tick-marks — uncounted,
  unexplained, textural (parking-lot logged; never mined).
- *Last Window* — the player keys the mast for the declination (dialogue scene with one
  player input: send). The writ broadcast stops after this — the every-bell repetition the
  player has lived with since ch. 11 goes silent, and the silence is worse.

**The count-lie (#1 of 2, design-critical)**: Corun's recovery runs in the real recovery UI;
mid-sequence the displayed digits begin disagreeing with the beat (the rhythm window drifts;
numerals repeat and skip); Pell's voice plays warm and close. Failure is authored and
unavoidable. The UI is never acknowledged afterward — no log entry, no menu note; the
recovery tutorial text never changes. Players who screenshot it are correct and alone.

**Ambient events & barks**: the sea wind's new register (Part III's bed introduced at the
cliff); the rotation's diminishing lamps up-trail (visible for a long scripted while); moths
absent (baseline now — no bark; the system remembers even if no one says it).

**Items, tokens & documents**: Pell's gloves (given away), the sealed pouch (gone home —
paper trail engine for Frame's act), the burned writ (ash interactable at the arch camp),
the watch log page (paper trail: "No fault found," underlined twice, unattributed).

**Audio priorities**: Pell's voice out of Corun — the ch. 3 voice-swap treatment, second and
last use before ch. 22; the count VO decohering (layered takes drifting out of sync — the
signature sound turned against itself); the stair's wind swallowing footsteps below; dawn-
bell arriving thin and formal after the worst night of Part II.
