# Chapter 5 — The Last Vent

**Part I: The Verge** · **Status: expanded treatment** · Departure day + the first week out. Hearthfall to
the last farm terraces to the first camp beyond warmth.

**Function**: The leaving. Rites, thresholds, and the first test of Kiln's command. The book's
last hour of safety, spent honestly.

## Beats

1. **The send-off.** Dawn-bell (there is no dawn; there is the bell). The route out of
   Hearthfall lined with people: not cheering — *watching*, the way you watch a funeral or a
   wedding, unsure which this is. The Order's blessing at the gate (Vestel's careful words);
   the Hall's simpler one (Line-Master Guyot: "Count your steps. Come back on the same
   number."); Odile at the third lamp-post, not waving, holding her elbows, which is worse than
   any waving. Iyo counts lamp-posts on the way out. The book notes the number. (It will
   matter to *her*, ch. 26 — not as a clue, as a habit.)
2. **The working caravan.** The Column finds its shape on the road: Kettle's stove-heart and
   the walking order around it; Tapper pacing the flank; watch rosters argued into being;
   Solene trailing an aerial from Kettle's mast, home's chatter still warm in her ears. The
   teamsters' culture (Pell and Corun's twin-act; Dovie's tuneless whistling; Nock's card
   games) — establish the eight lightly but *individually*: four of these people are being
   built for a safe exit, two for the long haul, two for graves, and the reader must not be
   able to tell which is which.
3. **Past the last vent.** The terraces end. The final vent tap — a roadside stone trough where
   travelers warm their hands, polished by six generations of palms — gets a small ceremony
   nobody planned: each of them touches it. Beyond: the first camp pitched in earnest cold, the
   first watch set, the first supper by allowance. The lamps at their backs: Hearthfall a smear
   of amber under the permanent evening. Elo takes the deep watch "so the others don't have
   to." Kiln allows it, over Adder's objection, with the only argument that matters: "He is the
   only one of us the night has already answered." Nobody sleeps well. Everybody sleeps.
4. **The test.** Day four: teamster Marta goes through weak snow-crust into a melt-channel —
   soaked to the waist, air at killing cold. The response is competence porn (Adder's cold
   drill, Brakes' stove discipline, Cistern's boiler talk while he works) — and then the
   command problem: her feet are damaged; she can walk in a week, haul in three. The Column is
   four days out. Turn her back with an escort (losing two more hauling bodies for a month) or
   carry her forward to heal on the move? Vell cites the allowance tables. Kiln hears everyone,
   then rules: she rides Kettle a week, works light, and *he* takes her hauling shifts folded
   into his own — a 260-year-old man pulling line. It is the correct decision and also theater,
   and it works, and Iyo clocks that it's theater and that it working is the point. Command
   style established: Kiln spends himself as currency.
5. **Close.** First sight, from a ridge, of the Gloaming ahead: the band where the Long Evening
   visibly fails, lamplight already necessary at noon-bell. Behind them, home's glow. Between,
   the road. Elo at the ridge line, looking neither way, waiting for them to finish looking.

## Character tracking

- **Kiln**: command as self-expenditure (the seed of ch. 21–22: he will spend all of it).
- **Adder vs. Elo**: the professional objection registered — Adder never trusts Elo, and the
  book never punishes Adder for it, because he isn't wrong, he's just not right either.
- **Marta**: her injury now, her honorable rotation home at ch. 12 — set up as mercy, not
  failure.
- **Solene**: last chapter of easy signal. Savor it.

## The dread beat

Quietest in the book, on purpose: Elo on watch, the camp asleep, and the line — stated once —
that with him sitting sentry, *nothing came near the light, and nothing had to.*

## Paper trail (interstitial after this chapter)

**Column supply allowance, day-zero table** (Vell's hand): rations, wick-hours, fuel, spares —
five months laid out in columns. At the bottom, the return-leg table, blank except its header.
Vell's tiny note: *"to be computed at the wreck."* The bureaucratic sentence that quietly admits
nobody knows if there's a return leg.

## Game notes

Act transition: the walk out of Hearthfall as playable procession (one long uncut traversal,
crowd density falling off to nothing). Camp systems tutorial (stove, watch, allowance). The
Marta decision: a first taste of expedition-management choice — or authored, if we keep systems
light; flag for design.

## Expanded treatment — content pass

**Decisions taken this pass**: the lamp-post-number candidate (111) is superseded by the ch. 1
style rule — the book never prints Iyo's totals; the send-off counts the lamp-posts *as an act*,
not as a figure. Marta keeps the cold-injury (locked; Ferro stays clean for the Reachgate
rotation).

### Scene list

- **S1 — Firstbell** (Hearthfall gate route · the Column, the crowd). The watching-not-cheering
  procession; Vestel's careful blessing; Guyot's better one ("Count your steps. Come back on
  the same number."). Turn: Odile at the third lamp-post, holding her elbows.
- **S2 — Finding the shape** (the out-road · all). Walking order argued into being around
  Kettle's warmth; Tapper's flank pattern; Solene trailing her aerial like a kite string home.
  Texture beats for all eight teamsters (two tics each, no backstory).
- **S3 — The last vent** (roadside trough · all). The unplanned ceremony: every palm to the
  warm stone, even Vell's, even — the Column pretending not to watch — Elo's. Turn: his palm
  leaves no mist on the stone. Stated once. (Rule-compliant: deniable, physical, unrepeated.)
- **S4 — First camp beyond warmth** (open ground · all). First watch roster; first allowance
  supper; Hearthfall a smear of amber behind. Turn: Kiln allowing Elo the deep watch over
  Adder's objection — "He is the only one of us the night has already answered."
- **S5 — Marta** (melt-channel, day four · Marta, Adder, Brakes, Cistern, Kiln, Vell). The
  crust failure; the competence sequence; the command problem argued in the open. Turn: Kiln
  folding her hauling shifts into his own. Theater, and correct, and Iyo clocking both.
- **S6 — The ridge** (day six · all). First sight of the Gloaming — lamplight needed at
  noon-bell on the road ahead. Exit: Elo at the ridge line, looking neither way, waiting for
  them to finish looking.

### Dialogue keys

- **Guyot** (the Hall's blessing): "Count your steps. Come back on the same number." — the
  whole book's contract, disguised as well-wishing; ch. 26 answers it.
- **Adder** (objecting): "He doesn't sleep. That's not a watchman, that's a window." /
  **Kiln**: "Then we are well glazed." — command by reframe; also genuinely funny; also not.
- **Marta** (soaked, teeth going): "I can walk." / **Cistern**, already cutting the boot:
  "You can also be wrong. One at a time."
- **Vell** (tables out): "The allowance says—" / **Kiln**: "The allowance is a guest at my
  fire, Auditor. It eats when we do." — their whole relationship in two lines; pays off when
  Vell's tables save the yoked columns in Part III.
- **Elo** (taking the deep watch): "Sleep. Nothing will come near the light." Beat. "It would
  be rude."

### Game content pack

**Locations**: the gate route (processional), the out-road (first travel segment), the last
vent trough (interactable — the palm ceremony is a one-button rite every party member
performs in sequence), first camp (camp-system tutorial), the melt-channel (scripted hazard),
the ridge viewpoint (act-transition vista).

**NPCs**: the crowd (Odile staged at the third lamp-post — findable, no dialogue, one
animation: holding her elbows), Vestel and Guyot at the gate, the Column entire.

**Side quests / optional content**:
- *The Shape of the March* — optional first-day errands that set the walking order: help
  Tapper mark flank cairns, run Solene's aerial, balance Sung's cook-crates. Choices set
  cosmetic marching positions the game keeps for the whole run (players notice in Part III
  when the order changes for grimmer reasons).
- *Palms* — the vent-trough rite: the player touches last; the game logs (silently) whether
  they watched Elo's turn. If they did: one extra line in ch. 23 ("you noticed early").
  If not: nothing. (Discontinuity-adjacent reward design: observation, never explanation.)
- *Marta's Boots* — post-rescue, dry her kit at the stove within the evening or she loses a
  toe (small, real consequence; she limps to the Reachgate either way — the rotation was
  always the plan; the toe is on the player).

**Ambient events & barks**: the crowd's silence broken by exactly one voice calling a
teamster's name (Ferro's mother); cindermoths thick at the last vent (their maximum density
in the game — the benchmark the absence will be measured against); first allowance grumble
comedy (Sung defending his ration cooking).

**Items, tokens & documents**: the day-zero allowance table (paper trail — the blank
return-leg header is a readable detail), the aerial spool, Marta's dried/ruined boots.

**Audio priorities**: the procession (feet, no voices, one name called); the vent trough's
under-stone thrum (heat as sound); the first camp's new quiet — Hearthfall's Hum gone,
nothing yet replacing it; Elo's watch: the loudest silence mix in Part I.
