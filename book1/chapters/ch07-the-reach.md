# Chapter 7 — The Reach

**Part II: The Grey Reach** · **Status: expanded treatment** · Weeks four–six. Into the Reach proper:
ruins of the Founders' retreat, first Provender signs.

**Function**: Open Part II's register — archaeology of their own trauma. The Column starts
walking the Founders' Walk backwards, reading 180-year-old panic in artifact form. Kiln's first
slip. The Reach's living dangers (human) announced by sign before they arrive in person.

## Beats

1. **Reading the road.** The retreat path is legible the way a battlefield is legible: cache
   cairns spaced a day apart, then half a day apart, then irregular (you can read the
   Founders' exhaustion in the spacing); shed weight in strata — furniture first, then tools,
   then, in one terrible kilometer, cradles and instrument cases and a piano's carcass like a
   whale rib. Kiln names things as they pass with quartermaster exactness ("Deck nine's loom
   benches. We argued three days about those"), and his narration is the chapter's spine: the
   only living memory of the cargo, walking through where it was abandoned.
2. **Cache ethics.** A sealed Founders' cache, intact: tools, wick, freeze-dried medical stock
   still viable. The debate: it is provision left "for those who come after" (Cistern's
   reading, and the plain inscription) — but the Column is provisioned, and the Reach has
   *residents* now (first mention: the Provender, scav lines, road-signs in an unfamiliar
   convention). Taking it is prudent and feels like grave-robbing; leaving it is pious and
   possibly feeds Saltcamp's market instead. Kiln's ruling threads it: take the medical stock,
   reseal the rest, log it. Vell logs it. (The log entry returns in ch. 24: on the way home
   they find the cache opened — by whom is never known, and the Column, changed, leaves their
   own surplus in it.)
3. **Graves.** The retreat's dead: cairn-rows at the camp sites, ship-plate markers with
   punched names. The Column's linewalker-and-priest double ritual forms here (Iyo squares the
   markers; Cistern says the shortened Office; it becomes their trail-habit, wordless). One
   marker stops Kiln: a name he knew — and here, the **first slip**: he calls Solene by that
   name, *Ossa*, asks her to "log the grave properly this time." Two seconds; he catches it;
   nobody comments (tone rule 2 in social form — the Column's courtesy is also the reader's
   first dread about him). Solene, nineteen, does the exactly right thing: answers to it,
   logs the grave.
4. **The Reach's residents.** Signs accumulating: fresh sledge-cuts crossing their path;
   a cairn re-stacked in the unfamiliar convention (Provender trail-code — Brakes starts
   compiling a glossary out of professional irritation); a distant light that paces them for
   half a day and then doesn't. And a Provender waymark at a fork, courteous and chilling in
   one object: an arrow of stamped therm-tokens nailed to a post — *this way to market* —
   with prices.
5. **Close.** Camp under an overhang the Founders also used (their soot still on the stone).
   Around the fire, the Column does what this book's people do with fear: inventory. Elo, asked
   directly by Dovie — first time anyone's asked him a real question in weeks — whether the
   road ahead is dangerous, gives the book's Elo-answer: "Not to me." Then, kindly, as if
   correcting an impression: "You'll be looked after." No one asks the follow-up. The fire
   pops. End chapter.

## Character tracking

- **Kiln**: the slip — degradation arrives *social* before it arrives operational. Track: ch. 7
  a name, ch. 13 a day, ch. 15 a memory that can't be his, ch. 21 the ship's parts perfect
  while people's names go.
- **Solene**: answering-to-Ossa = her quiet graduation from kid to crew.
- **Brakes**: the trail-code glossary — her coping is competence with jokes on top.
- **Elo**: "You'll be looked after" — comfort and threat, unresolvable, per speech rules.

## The dread beat

Elo's answer. Secondary: the piano's carcass (dread of scale — what people will shed to keep
walking; the reader does the math about what the Column would shed).

## Paper trail (interstitial after this chapter)

**Brakes' Provender trail-code glossary, v1** — practical, wrong in two entries (corrected in a
later hand, ch. 24), with marginalia: *"tokens on a post = menu. we are on it."*

## Game notes

The Reach = act-2 open structure: the retreat path as the "main road" with legible strata
(environmental narrative at walking pace), caches as risk/reward pickups with a
reputation cost systemized or authored, Provender waymarks as literal wayfinding UI diegesis.
Kiln's slip should be a bark the player can miss — no cutscene framing.

### Dialogue keys

- **Kiln** (reading spacing): "Day-camps here. Then half-days. Here they stopped burying and
  started marking. You can read a retreat like a pulse."
- **Cistern** (cache debate): "It says *for those who come after.* We are those. That's not
  theology, Warrant Officer, it's addressing."
- **Vell** (logging): "Withdrawn: medical, 11 kilos. Basis: need. Authority: Kiln. Witness:
  all of you." — reading his own entry aloud so the ethics have a record; his defining habit.
- **Brakes** (glossary, muttering): "Two stones crossed means toll. Three means *toll went
  badly for someone.* Lovely country."
- **Elo** (the exit line, locked from treatment): "Not to me." / "You'll be looked after."

### Game content pack

**Locations**: the retreat path (travel segments with authored strata: furniture field, tool
field, the cradle kilometer, the piano), the sealed cache (interior interaction space), the
cairn-row campsite, the overhang camp (Founders' soot on the ceiling — lie-on-back viewable).

**NPCs**: none met — the Reach's residents exist this chapter as *signs* (design rule). The
pacing light is an unresolvable ambient (never sourced, never repeated).

**Side quests / optional content**:
- *The Cache Ledger* — full cache interaction: itemize before withdrawing (the player performs
  Vell's ethic mechanically); resealing properly is a small craft interaction; done right, the
  ch. 24 return-state shows the Column's own surplus added and a stranger's tally mark added
  beneath Vell's.
- *Marker Duty* — squaring grave-markers along the route (repeatable micro-verb; each squared
  marker fires one line of Kiln's memory — a name, a trade, once a joke). Optional, unlimited,
  unrewarded. Players who do them all get nothing but the memories. Correct.
- *The Glossary* — collect trail-code sightings to fill Brakes' glossary (v1 has two wrong
  entries by design; Saltcamp NPCs correct them if the player shows it around — literacy
  through embarrassment).

**Ambient events & barks**: the pacing light (one half-day travel segment, never explained);
sledge-cut fresh/old reading (Adder teaches it in barks); the cradle kilometer (all barks
suppressed for its duration — the audio does the grief).

**Items, tokens & documents**: medical stock (real supply value), the glossary v1 (usable
document that upgrades), cache manifest page, the therm-token arrow (one token pryable —
Saltcamp currency preview; taking it is noted by nobody and remembered by the toll-clan's
ledger in ch. 11: "one token, returned or owed").

**Audio priorities**: wind through hull-plate markers (each cairn a slightly different chord —
the Reach's sound signature); the piano's strings still faintly live in gusts (one sting,
found not scored); Kiln's narration recorded close and dry.
