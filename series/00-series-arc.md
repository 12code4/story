# NIGHTWARD — The Series Arc (Books 1–4)

Four books, one escalation logic: each book changes the **relationship** between the light and
the dark, and each book *inverts something structural* from the one before — direction, border,
or grammar. The mystery policy holds at series scale: answers are bought, they arrive sideways,
they cost, and every one opens a bigger unpaid question. No book ends on an explainer.

| # | Title | One line | Direction | The inversion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Walked** | An expedition crosses the night to salvage the cores and finds the missing tending the sleepers | Into the dark | (baseline) |
| 2 | **The Woken** | The counting completes and the dark begins *delivering the colony* — twenty thousand sleepers, escorted to a Verge that cannot absorb them | The dark comes to the light | Journey → border; expedition → institution |
| 3 | **The Burned** | The light keeps thinning; a mixed column crosses the Glare to find out what's drinking the day | Into the light | Cold → heat; lamps → shade; the survival game inverted whole |
| 4 | **The Kept** | Every covenant matures at once; the question of the whole series: can a people be kept without being owned | Convergence | Question → answer; guest → keeper |

## The escalation (what each book is *about*)

- **Book 1 — contact.** Denial → protocol. Personal scale: one expedition, one sister. Ends:
  the border exists and has manners.
- **Book 2 — arrival.** Protocol → cohabitation. Civic scale: the true colony wakes and is
  delivered in files across the cold-line; the Verge's institutions (and its grief-culture)
  crack under a *fulfilled promise*. Ends: the dark, its contract complete, asks the Verge —
  as a polity, for the first time — a new question.
- **Book 3 — the other neighbor.** Cohabitation → triage. Planetary scale: the thinning light
  was never the night's doing; the day side has a resident too, and its grammar is not
  invitation. A mixed column (Verge, returned, woken) crosses the Glare. Ends: the world has
  two borders, and humanity lives between them, on terms it helped set, at a price it paid.
- **Book 4 — the household.** Triage → settlement. Origin scale: Meridian, the diversion, the
  bargain struck in the dying descent, the identity of Ghost, the meaning of the off-by-one —
  the purchased answers, and the ones the series declines with honor. Ends: the final form of
  the theme — keeping without owning — enacted by every faction at once, or refused.

## The truth braid — WORKING PROPOSAL (not locked; per `book1/06-deferred.md`, we decide together)

Recommended braid: **A + C** (the Custodian + the Contract). Something native, patient, and
exact lives beneath the night. In the Errand's dying descent, a bargain was struck — the
sleepers' twenty thousand lives kept, against payment on schedule: tending labor, warmth, and
grief, delivered by the Walked. "The counting" is that contract maturing. **Who struck the
bargain is the series' held card** (candidates: the ship herself; the captain; someone stranger
— decided no earlier than Book 3's drafting).

Acceptance test (from the parking lot) and how the braid pays it:
- *The counting* → the contract's amortization; completes in Book 2 as **delivery**.
- *The work-errand deviation* (Elo → Kiln) → the contract needed its last officer logged
  aboard to close the crew ledger; Book 2 confirms by consequence, never by exposition.
- *The clean signal / "we hear everything"* → the night carries what it tends; reception
  without reciprocity is a term of custody. Book 2 institutionalizes it ("the answering").
- *The thinning light* → **deliberately NOT the night's doing.** The braid assigns it to the
  day side (Book 3's engine) — the one big misdirection the series permits itself, honest
  because Book 1 never once claimed otherwise.
- *The off-by-one* → held to Book 4. Candidates: a keeper assigned to her; the contract's
  audit; something that loved the count itself. (Do not decide before Book 4.)

Fallback braids kept alive: **B** (the Other Cargo) can replace A with minimal Book 2 damage
(the custodian becomes the ship's seed-mind — colder, more sci-fi); **D** (pure ecology)
remains the horror-purist option and would force Book 4 into tragedy. Flag at each book's
gate which braids remain compatible; kill options late, not early.

## Motif ledger across books (binding continuity)

- **The count**: broken (B1) → institutionalized, the new rote (B2) → shared with the woken,
  who count differently and are right differently (B3) → answered (B4; the series' last line
  belongs to it).
- **Lamps/windows**: kept against the dark (B1) → kept *for* arrivals (B2) → shaded against
  the day (B3) → kept, finally, for everyone at once (B4).
- **Invitations**: the dark's grammar (B1) → learned by home (B2) → discovered absent on the
  day side, which is worse (B3) → extended *by* humanity for the first time (B4).
- **Manifests/ledgers**: the Manifest of the Missing (B1) → the Arrival Rolls (B2) → the
  Sunward Muster (B3) → one ledger, closed correct (B4).
- **The Hum**: home's pitch (B1) → taught to the woken as *their* ship's voice (B2) → absent
  in the Glare; its lack navigable (B3) → the whole world's pitch, kept by many hands (B4).
- **Ribbons**: two on one wrist (B1) → one in a drawer, one on a lamp (B1 end) → worn by two
  sisters working one border (B2) → carried sunward (B3) → the drawer opened last (B4).

## Game-franchise notes

Each book is a game with the same bones and one inverted core system: B1 heat-and-dark
expedition (built first); B2 a border-institution narrative (processing, housing, triage —
management-genre hybridization is an option, flagged, not assumed); B3 the survival game
mirrored (heat as enemy, shade-hopping traversal, light-discipline); B4 synthesis. Shared
tech: the count verb, the radio, manifest-as-questlog, the paper trail. The Book 1 game's
data pipeline (chapters as scene scripts) is the franchise's production spine.
